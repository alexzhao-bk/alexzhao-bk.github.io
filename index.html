I am a PhD student in Computational Biology at the University of California, Berkeley, advised by Prof. Nilah Ioannidis and Prof. Bin Yu. My research focuses on developing reliable and interpretable deep learning models for genomics, with applications in molecular and physiological phenotype prediction, variant effect prediction, and regulatory DNA sequence design. More broadly, I am interested in using these models to better understand gene regulation and inform therapeutic development.

Prior to Berkeley, I was a Senior Assistant Scientist at Regeneron Pharmaceuticals. I hold an M.S. in Computational Biology from Carnegie Mellon University, where I was honored to work with and be advised by Prof. Russell Schwartz, and a B.A. in Mathematics and Statistics from Grinnell College.
